Privacy Policy — Last updated: April 2026

Grace ("we," "our," or "us") is operated from Switzerland and is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Grace app. Grace complies with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is Grace, based in Switzerland. For any questions or requests regarding your data, you can reach us at getgraceapp@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email from the identity provider along with a cryptographic token used to verify your identity. We do not receive or store your Apple or Google password. This information is used solely to personalize your experience and identify your account.

Journal & Reflection Data

Your journal entries, prayers, gratitude notes, voice recordings, and attached photos are stored exclusively on your device using iOS-protected local storage. This content is never uploaded to our servers and is never accessible to us. It is private and visible only to you. Journal entries are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM encryption, with the encryption key stored in the iOS Keychain (hardware-protected secure storage).

Meditation Session Metadata

When you complete a reflection, affirmation, or meditation session, we store basic metadata about the session on our servers — specifically the session type (e.g. guided meditation, affirmation, verse reflection), the duration, whether it was completed, and the date. This data is associated with your account and is used to track your personal progress and streaks.

Pause Activity

When Grace displays a mindful pause, a timestamp is recorded locally on your device. Pause counts and history are stored on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.

Device Identifier

When you first create an account, we collect your device's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) — a random identifier assigned by iOS that is unique to our app on your device. This identifier is sent to our servers once, solely to prevent abuse of the free trial (one trial per device). It is not an advertising identifier and cannot be used to track you across other apps.

App Monitoring Preferences

When you choose to monitor specific apps in Settings, your selections are stored locally on your device only as privacy-preserving opaque tokens provided by iOS. These tokens cannot be decoded to identify specific apps outside of your device. This preference is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. It is used solely to show you a mindful pause screen when you open one of those apps.

Camera & Photo Library

If you choose to attach a photo to a journal entry, Grace requests access to your camera or photo library. Photos you attach are stored only on your device as part of your journal entry. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. You may revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera / Photos.

Microphone & Voice Notes

If you choose to record a voice note in a journal entry, Grace requests access to your microphone. Voice recordings are stored only on your device as part of your journal entry. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party. You may revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Biometric Authentication

If you enable Face ID, authentication is handled entirely by iOS and Apple's Secure Enclave. Grace never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data. We only receive a success or failure result from the operating system.

2. App Monitoring & Screen Time

Grace includes a voluntary mindful interruption feature. When you open a distracting app that you have selected in Settings, Grace displays a gentle pause screen encouraging reflection before you continue.

This feature is entirely voluntary. You choose which apps to monitor. Grace does not restrict access to any app. All interruptions are configured by you and can be disabled or removed at any time.

User Authorization

Before this feature can activate, Grace requests your explicit permission through iOS's native Screen Time authorization dialog. Grace uses Apple's individual authorization mode (FamilyControls with the .individual member type), meaning you — the device owner — authorize the feature for your own device. This is not a parental control feature and does not involve any other person's device. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Use of Apple Screen Time APIs

Grace uses Apple's Screen Time and FamilyControls frameworks — including DeviceActivityMonitor, DeviceActivityReport, ShieldConfiguration, and ShieldAction — solely to enable this mindful pause feature.

How it works:

  • When you select apps to monitor, iOS provides Grace with privacy-preserving opaque tokens representing those apps. Grace cannot decode these tokens into identifiable app names or bundle IDs that could be transmitted to our servers. Your app selections are never disclosed to Grace outside of your device.
  • The opaque tokens for apps you have chosen to monitor are stored in your device's secure local storage only and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Grace uses Apple's DeviceActivityMonitor extension to detect when one of those apps is opened. This detection is performed entirely on your device by iOS. No information identifying which app was opened is ever stored, transmitted, or linked to your identity.
  • When a monitored app is opened, iOS presents a shield overlay powered by ShieldConfiguration — a native system interface rendered entirely by iOS, not by Grace — prompting you to pause and reflect. Grace cannot intercept, access, or read any content from the monitored app.
  • If you choose to reflect, the ShieldAction extension signals the Grace app to begin a wellness session. If you choose to continue, the shield is immediately dismissed and the app opens normally.
  • DeviceActivityReport may be used to generate on-device summaries of your mindful pauses (for example, how many times you paused this week). This data is processed and displayed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • No screen time data, usage statistics, or behavioral data is stored, transmitted, or used outside of on-device processing.
  • Grace does not monitor the content of any app or website.
  • Grace does not track you across apps or websites, and this data is never used for advertising or profiling.

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising purposes.

  • Consent — for optional features you explicitly enable, such as Apple Health integration, notifications, app monitoring, camera/microphone access, and biometric authentication. You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Storage

Account and Profile Data

Your account information (name, email, onboarding preferences, and meditation session metadata) is stored using Supabase, a cloud infrastructure provider that uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Journal Entries and Voice Notes

Your journal entries, attached photos, and voice recordings are stored exclusively on your device using iOS-protected local storage. They are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Subscription Management

Subscription billing is handled by RevenueCat, a third-party billing service. To process and verify your subscription, your anonymized user ID (a randomly generated identifier with no connection to your name or email) is shared with RevenueCat solely for this purpose. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, journal content, or any other personal data. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Authentication Providers

If you sign in with Apple or Google, your authentication is processed through those providers' servers. We receive only your name, email, and a verification token. We do not receive your password. Apple and Google's respective privacy policies apply to data processed by their services.

Audio Content

Grace streams guided meditation and ambient audio from a content delivery network (CDN). Your device's IP address may be visible to the CDN provider in the course of standard HTTPS requests. No personal data or account information is included in these requests.

Analytics

Grace uses PostHog, an analytics provider with EU data hosting, to collect pseudonymous app usage data. Analytics events are routed through our Supabase infrastructure before reaching PostHog's EU servers. PostHog receives your anonymized account ID and event data only. PostHog does not receive your name, email, journal content, or any other personal information. You can review PostHog's privacy policy at posthog.com/privacy.

Error Monitoring

Grace uses Sentry, an error monitoring service, to collect crash reports and diagnostic logs. Sentry may receive error messages, device information, and app version data. Sentry does not receive your name, email, journal content, or any other personal data. Session replays are disabled. You can review Sentry's privacy policy at sentry.io/privacy.

Local Data

App monitoring preferences, notification settings, pause history, favorite quotes, and voice preferences are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

International Data Transfers

Grace is operated from Switzerland. Some of the third-party services we use (Supabase, RevenueCat, PostHog, Apple, Google) may process data on servers located outside Switzerland and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where such transfers occur, they are protected by appropriate safeguards including standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), or the service provider's participation in recognized data protection frameworks.

5. Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health integration, Grace may read your existing mindful minutes and write new mindful session data to your Apple Health app. This data:

  • Is used only within Grace to enrich your wellness tracking
  • Is never transmitted to our servers
  • Is never shared with any third party
  • Can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices

6. Analytics

Grace collects pseudonymous usage analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve the experience. We use PostHog, a privacy-focused analytics provider with EU data hosting, to process this data.

What we collect:

  • App opens and session activity (e.g. meditation started, meditation completed, journal entry saved)
  • Session metadata: meditation type, duration, and whether it was completed
  • Journal entry metadata only: entry type, whether a mood or photo was attached, and approximate length category (short, medium, or long). Journal text content is never collected.
  • Subscription status (trial, active, or expired)
  • Whether the app monitoring feature is enabled
  • Paywall interactions (shown, purchased)

What we do NOT collect:

  • Journal text, prayers, or reflection content
  • Names, emails, or other personally identifying information
  • Photos, voice notes, or any media content
  • Specific apps you have chosen to monitor
  • Your location, IP address, or device advertising identifier

Analytics events are transmitted through our Supabase infrastructure (the same secure servers that store your account data) and forwarded to PostHog's EU servers. Your analytics data is pseudonymously associated with your account ID. PostHog cannot link this ID to your name or email.

You can request deletion of your analytics data at any time by contacting getgraceapp@gmail.com. When you delete your account, your analytics data is disassociated from your identity. You may also request explicit deletion of analytics data by contacting us.

PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy

7. Notifications

If you opt into notifications, Grace stores your notification preferences locally on your device. Notification content (morning verse, evening reflection) is generated locally and not transmitted to our servers.

8. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Update your name or email in Settings > Account
  • Remove monitored apps in Settings > Monitored Apps
  • Revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings → Screen Time
  • Disable biometric lock in Settings > Security
  • Delete your account and all associated data in Settings > Delete Account
  • Contact us to request a full export of your data in a portable format

Under the Swiss FADP and EU GDPR, you also have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data (see account deletion below).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent — for any processing based on consent, at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) at edoeb.admin.ch, or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence if you are in the EU/EEA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at getgraceapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes all data associated with your account from our servers within 30 days. Please note that journal entries, voice notes, photos, and other locally stored data remain on your device after account deletion because they were never uploaded to our servers. You can remove this data by deleting the app from your device.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data and meditation session metadata on our servers for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated server-side data is permanently removed within 30 days. Locally stored data (journal entries, preferences, pause history) persists on your device until you delete the app.

Analytics data collected by PostHog is retained for 12 months and is automatically deleted thereafter. Error logs collected by Sentry are retained for 30 days.
