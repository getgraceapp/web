Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Grace → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

Where are my journal entries stored?

Your journal entries are encrypted and stored exclusively on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, your journal entries will be removed from your device.

How does the Screen Time feature work?

Grace can gently pause distracting apps you choose and invite you to reflect before continuing. This uses Apple's Screen Time framework in individual mode — you control which apps are monitored, and you can disable it at any time in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Open Grace → Settings → scroll to the bottom → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data from our servers within 30 days.

How do I export my data?

Open Grace → Settings → Export Data. This generates a ZIP file containing your profile, meditation sessions, journal entries, and preferences.
